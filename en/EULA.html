Welcome

Build around our work — with confidence.

Shadow Haven Studios (“SHS”) and Shadow Haven Hosting (“SHH”) create tools, systems, mods, assets, and services for the Arma Reforger community.

We want people to enjoy our work, customize it for their communities, and build useful extensions around it. This Agreement is intended to make those freedoms clear while protecting the original SHS projects, files, branding, and development work that make those extensions possible.

The basic idea is simple
  • You may use SHS Content, configure it, and build original add-ons that depend on it.
  • You may make reasonable private changes for your server or development team.
  • You may publish compatibility patches, integrations, and permitted visual extensions.
  • You may not reupload, extract, repackage, resell, or present SHS Content as your own.
  • Public extensions should continue to require the official SHS release as a dependency.
  • Commercial use requires written permission.

Please read the full Agreement below. The plain-language summary is provided for convenience, but the complete terms control.

Community Extensions

We welcome original community projects that work with SHS Content.

You may create and publish an Extension when:

  • The Extension does not operate as a replacement for the Original Content.

Permission to create an Extension does not permit you to copy, repackage, reupload, or publish a modified version of SHS Content.

Official Dependency Requirement

A publicly distributed Extension based on SHS Content must use the official SHS Content as a required dependency whenever reasonably possible.

The dependency should direct users to an authorized official distribution of the Original Content.

You may not intentionally remove, conceal, bypass, or replace the dependency in order to:

  • Avoid use of the official SHS release.
  • Create an unauthorized independent fork.

  • Divert users to an unauthorized mirror or reupload.

Temporary technical fallback behavior is permitted, but an Extension may not contain copied SHS Content for the purpose of permanently replacing the official dependency.

Compatibility Patches

You may create and publish a compatibility patch that helps SHS Content work with another mod, framework, server system, or gameplay environment.

A compatibility patch must:

  • Require the official SHS Content as a dependency.
  • Contain only the patch developer’s original material or lawfully licensed third-party material.
  • Avoid bundling protected SHS Content.
  • Avoid copying substantial SHS code.
  • Use overrides or separate dependencies instead of modified SHS files whenever reasonably possible.
  • Identify any other required dependencies.
  • Avoid implying that SHS guarantees compatibility or provides support for the patch.

When direct modification of an SHS file is technically unavoidable, public distribution of that modified file requires prior written permission.
